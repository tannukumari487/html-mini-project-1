Vegetable Pulao Recipe

A simple delicious homemade meal.

About Dish

vegetable Pulao is a tasty rice dish made with vegetables and spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1 cup mix vegetables
  • 2 cup water
  • salt and spices

Instructions

  1. wash the rice
  2. Prepare the vegetables
  3. Cook the rice with vegetables and spices.
  4. serve the pulao hot.

Tip:Adjust the spices according to taste.
